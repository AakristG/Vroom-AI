# Toyota model photos

Real photographs from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/), used under the free licenses listed below.
They are representative photos of each nameplate, not official Toyota images, and the generation shown may differ from the model year being browsed.
Resized to 1280 px wide; otherwise unmodified. `manifest.json` holds the same data and is read by the app (and copied to `backend/apps/vehicles/model_photos.json`).

Regenerate or add models with `py -I frontend/scripts/fetch_model_photos.py frontend/public/media/models [slug ...]`.
